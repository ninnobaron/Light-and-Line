About me

From early sketches filling notebooks to a formal education in Graphic Design at the University of Manitoba, my artistic inclinations have always been a guiding force. This journey has honed my eye for aesthetics and my ability to translate creative visions into compelling designs. In fact, many of my professors at the University of Manitoba have often commented on my innate eye and talent for the design field, a validation that fuels my commitment to this craft.
